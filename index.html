About & Privacy

About

Catch That Word is a vocabulary game. A meaning appears at the top and you catch the falling word that matches it.

There are 500 words across five levels, from everyday words up to national spelling bee finals, and 20 pets to collect along the way.

It was built by A.Lundi and is free to play.

Privacy

This game does not collect any personal information.

There are no accounts, no sign-ups, and no email addresses.

Your pets, extra lives, and progress are saved in your own browser using localStorage. That data stays on your device and is never sent anywhere.

There are no cookies, no analytics, and no advertising.

The game makes no requests to any other website.

The “Speak to me” button uses your browser’s built-in voice. No recordings are made or stored.

Clearing your browser data will erase your saved pets.
